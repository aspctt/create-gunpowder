<p>Create: Gunpowder adds production chains to produce gunpowder, mainly for modpacks or worlds where creepers don’t exist or drop it. Instead of mob farms, gunpowder becomes a processed resource that fits naturally into Create’s progression.</p>

<h3>Production</h3>

<p>Press Flint --> Flint Shard</p>

<p>Mill Coal or Charcoal --> 2x Carbon Dust</p>

<p>Mill Sand or Sandstone --> 2x Mineral Dust</p>

<p>Mix Flint Shard + Carbon Dust --> Volatile Powder</p>

<p>Mix Volatile Powder + Mineral Dust + Water + Heat --> 2x Gunpowder</p>
